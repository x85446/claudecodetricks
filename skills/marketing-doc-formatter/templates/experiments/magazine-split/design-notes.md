# magazine-split

- Magazine-style layout: large orange-accented pull quote headline, then body
- Three-column lower section for core capabilities (denser than 2-col)
- Bold cobalt initial-letter drop cap on the intro paragraph for visual interest
- Small caps section headers
- Tables remain standard brand style for consistency
- Feels editorial / thoughtful; contrasts the corporate/technical feel of other variants
- Good for blog-style marketing PDFs or longer-form intros
