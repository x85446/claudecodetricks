Building custom IoT device management infrastructure takes months to years. Izuma Device Management compresses that timeline from years to a day — a proven platform (originally Arm Pelion / mbed Cloud) handling secure connectivity, firmware updates, and fleet operations from ultra-constrained MCUs to full edge servers.

Core Capabilities

Secure Device Connectivity

LwM2M/CoAP with DTLS. X.509 mTLS. TPM 2.0, TrustZone, PARSEC. Protocol translation for BLE, Zigbee, Z-Wave, BACnet, Modbus.

Firmware Updates (FOTA)

One-shot, continuous, mesh campaigns. Delta updates. Encrypted/signed images. Resume after disconnect.

Fleet Management

Directory scales from dozens to millions. Filter, group, suspend, bulk-operate. Custom attributes.

Edge Computing & Monitoring

True Kubernetes. Offline containers. SD-WAN. Device Sentry incidents. Remote log/terminal.
