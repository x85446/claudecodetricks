Izuma Device Management

Chip-to-Cloud IoT at Scale

Building custom IoT device management infrastructure takes months to years and diverts engineering from product differentiation. Izuma Device Management compresses that timeline from years to a day — a proven platform with over a decade of evolution (originally Arm Pelion / mbed Cloud) that handles secure connectivity, firmware updates, and fleet operations from ultra-constrained MCUs to full edge servers. SOC 2 certified.

Core Capabilities

Secure Device Connectivity

LwM2M/CoAP with DTLS. X.509 mTLS. Hardware root of trust (TPM 2.0, TrustZone, PARSEC). Protocol translation for BLE, Zigbee, Z-Wave, BACnet, Modbus. Outbound HTTPS only.

Firmware Updates (FOTA)

One-shot, continuous, and mesh campaigns. Delta updates. Encrypted and signed images. Resume after disconnect. Scales from MCU binaries to multi-GB images.

Fleet Management

Device Directory scales from dozens to millions. Filter, group, suspend, bulk-operate. First-to-claim enrollment. Custom attributes.

Edge Computing & Monitoring

True Kubernetes (not a fork). Offline container operation. SD-WAN with firewall traversal. Device Sentry threshold-based incidents. Remote log collection and terminal.
