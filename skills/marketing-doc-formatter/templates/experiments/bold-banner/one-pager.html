Izuma Device Management

Chip-to-Cloud IoT at Scale

Building custom IoT device management infrastructure takes months to years. Izuma Device Management compresses that timeline to days — a proven platform with over a decade of evolution (originally Arm Pelion / mbed Cloud) handling secure connectivity, firmware updates, and fleet operations from ultra-constrained MCUs to edge servers. SOC 2 certified.

Core Capabilities

Secure Device Connectivity

LwM2M/CoAP with DTLS. X.509 mTLS. Hardware root of trust (TPM 2.0, TrustZone, PARSEC). Protocol translation for legacy devices.

Firmware Updates (FOTA)

One-shot, continuous, mesh campaigns. Delta updates. Encrypted and signed images. Resume after disconnect.

Fleet Management

Directory scales from dozens to millions. Filter, group, suspend, bulk-operate. Custom attributes.

Edge Computing & Monitoring

True Kubernetes. Offline containers. SD-WAN. Device Sentry threshold-based incidents. Remote terminal.
